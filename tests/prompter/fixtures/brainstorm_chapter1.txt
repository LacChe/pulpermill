## Chapter 1 Options: The Cold Hearthstone

Here are three options for Chapter 1, building from the established situation and focusing on Pim Tallowby. Each option utilizes a different combination of tie and object/rule, and outlines a concrete aim for Pim within that context.

**Option 1: Applewood and Approval (Tie: Pim Tallowby & Orla Fennick; Object: Applewood Bundles)**

Pim aims to secure the last bundle of golden applewood from Old Man Tiber’s cart, hoping a particularly beautiful loaf baked with it will impress Orla Fennick. Orla wants a steady supply of quality applewood to maintain the bakery’s reputation and prove her own skill in reading the loaves. The clash comes when Corbin Reeds also eyes the same bundle, charming Tiber with a story about mapping the best apple trees, while Pim stumbles over her words, trying to offer a trade of three sun-marked loaves. Losing the applewood means a less-than-stellar loaf and another chance for Orla to dismiss her efforts as ‘cute’, costing Pim a step towards being taken seriously. Pim ultimately secures the bundle by offering Tiber a loaf *and* promising to sketch his prize-winning hen, leveraging her knack for quick sketches to outmaneuver Corbin’s storytelling.

**Option 2: Newcomer's Loaf and Company (Tie: Corbin Reeds & Pim Tallowby; Rule: Loaves must be shared with newcomers)**

Pim aims to bake a perfect loaf specifically for Corbin Reeds, hoping to solidify their friendship and enjoy his company at the upcoming Harvest Fair. Corbin wants Pim’s companionship, but a family of travelling tinkers arrives in Thimble Hollow, demanding a share of the morning's baking as is custom. The clash occurs when Pim hesitates sharing her best loaf with the newcomers, wanting to save it for Corbin, leading him to notice her slight snub and question her generosity. Losing Corbin’s favour means missing out on stimulating conversation and a chance to feel seen beyond the bakery, costing her a small but significant pleasure. She compromises by offering the tinkers a slightly smaller loaf, baked with extra poppyseeds - an offering that pleases both newcomers and Corbin, who appreciates the nuance.

**Option 3: Reliable Rise and a Fickle Oven (Tie: Orla Fennick & Pim Tallowby; Rule: The Weather Oven must be fired with applewood only)**

Pim aims to prove her reliability by baking a full batch of wind-marked loaves despite the continuing fog and the slightly damp applewood Orla has rationed for the day. Orla needs a steady output of correctly marked loaves to appease the villagers, who rely on them for their farming schedules. The clash arrives when Pim struggles to get the loaves to rise properly with the slightly less-than-ideal wood, frustrating Orla who expects a seamless performance. Failing to deliver a full batch signifies a lack of competence and reinforces Orla’s perception of Pim as needing constant supervision, costing her a chance to take on more responsibility. She manages to salvage the bake by adding a pinch of wild thyme to the dough, coaxing a better rise, demonstrating initiative that even Orla acknowledges with a rare grunt of approval.
